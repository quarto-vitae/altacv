$-- AltaCV has no dedicated single-line entry macro. \cvachievement (an icon,
$-- a bold header and a line of description) gives the closest "one line, no
$-- elaboration" shape brief calls for. The icon is a field \cvachievement
$-- expects but the conventional shape doesn't supply, so a fixed award medal
$-- stands in for it: brief best suits awards and memberships. `where` is
$-- preferred over `with` for the description, falling back to it, and `when`
$-- is appended in parentheses when given.
$for(entries)$
\cvachievement
  {\faAward}
  {$entries.what$} % what
  {$if(entries.where)$$entries.where$$else$$entries.with$$endif$$if(entries.when)$ ($entries.when$)$endif$}

$sep$\divider
$endfor$
